Age Calculator

The Age Calculator can determine the age or interval between two dates. The calculated age is displayed in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.

This calculator follows the most commonly used age system where a person's age increases on their birthday.

In some countries and cultures, age is calculated differently. This calculator uses the international standard method.

How Age is Calculated

Age is calculated as the difference between the selected Date of Birth and the selected comparison date.

This calculator follows the standard international age calculation method where age increases on a person's birthday.

Leap years are automatically considered while calculating age.
